About FISMMOK LOGISTIX NIGERIA LIMITED

Your trusted corporate gateway to seamless clearing, forwarding, import-export facilitation, and container haulage across Nigeria's vital trade gateways.

Our Foundation

Rooted in Apapa, Connecting Nigeria to Global Trade

FISMMOK LOGISTIX NIGERIA LIMITED was founded with a singular, resolute objective: to bring unassailable transparency, speed, and regulatory excellence to the Nigerian maritime and supply chain landscape.

Headquartered strategically along the bustling commercial arteries of Apapa, Lagos—home to Nigeria's largest seaports—we maintain hands-on physical access to terminal quays, customs assessment commands, and shipping line agencies. This eliminates the bureaucratic friction and costly delays that routinely burden Nigerian importers and exporters.

Whether handling containerized general merchandise, perishable agricultural export consignments, heavy industrial machinery, or high-priority air cargo, our seasoned team orchestrates every phase from customs documentation (Form M, PAAR, CCVO) to gate-out and final destination offloading.

The Nigerian Logistics Challenge We Solve

  • •
    Demurrage & Storage Penalties: We commence pre-clearing prior to vessel discharge, securing immediate gate-out within free terminal storage windows.
  • •
    Tariff Classification & HS Codes: Precise customs classification prevents queries, audit holds, and demand notices.
  • •
    Interstate Haulage Breakdowns: We deploy road-worthy, insured, GPS-monitored trucks with certified drivers across all geopolitical zones.
