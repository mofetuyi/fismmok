Accredited NCS Agency

Customs Clearance & Brokerage

Nigerian port customs clearance demands rigorous regulatory expertise. FISMMOK LOGISTIX is an accredited customs broker operating directly across Apapa Area 1 Command, Tin Can Island Command, and Lillypond. We manage Form M processing, Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR) issuance, SONCAP/NAFDAC documentation, physical and non-intrusive scanning, and swift customs examination.

  • Fast-track single window customs declarations
  • Accurate HS Code tariff classification and duty assessment
  • Proactive pre-clearing to prevent demurrage penalties
