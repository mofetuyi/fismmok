Nigerian Port Process Transparency

Understanding Your Cargo Journey

We keep every stage transparent so our clients can prepare sales channels and avoid costly demurrage.

1

Pre-Arrival Assessment (PAAR)

Commercial invoices and CCVO are submitted to Nigerian Customs for risk assessment and duty assessment issuance before the ship drops anchor.

2

Berthing & Terminal Discharge

Vessel discharges containers at Apapa Port quays (APMT, TICT) or Tin Can Island. Consignee manifest is validated with the Nigerian Ports Authority.

3

Customs Examination & Release

Physical or non-intrusive container scanning by NCS officers. Upon clearance stamping, shipping line delivery order (DO) and terminal charges are settled.

4

Terminal Gate-Out & Haulage

Terminal release authorization issued; flatbed truck enters the terminal, mounts container, and commences transit with real-time GPS tracking to the receiver.
